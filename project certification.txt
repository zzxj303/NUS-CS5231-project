Project Clarification
Hi all, 

The course project follows the same high-level education principle we had in the previous two modules. It is concept and design-oriented, light in implementation, which fits the new structure of our lecture of this semester. Here is a summary and clarification of what we have disccuss in last week's lecture. 

Your goal is to apply concepts and security thinking from Module 1 or Module 2 to a new problem. You can use the problem you chose or the LLM agent skill problem we have released as a reference. The focus is to think about how a new security problem can be understood in terms of problems and techniques we have already studied.

Planned schedule
Weeks 11–12: Problem definition and analysis
Weeks 12–13: Solution design and description
One additional week: Implementation
Grading
Problem statement and analysis: 10 marks
Technical design: 10 marks
Implementation: 5 marks
Individual report: 5 marks
Most marks are allocated for analysis and design, not implementation. A simple proof-of-concept implementation is acceptable, and you can use AI tools, there are tools and AI tokens available through NUS and SoC. 

Reports and submission
Your group project report needs to include the following components:

As the Section 1, include a 1–2 page executive summary covering the problem, its connection to Module 1 or 2, your technical design, the extensions needed, and preliminary evidence supporting your idea. This part is intended to be the raw communication from the team.

Specifically, you need to answer these questions in short and concise sentences:

Problem statement and analysis.

What is the problem statement?
How is it similar a problem to one we have learned in Module 1 or Module 2?
Technical design

How do you design the solution based on ideas we have learned in Module 1 or Module 2?
What is the difference and the extension you need to make to address the new problem?
Section 2 and onwards is a technical report with an introduction, background, problem definition, technical design, evaluation, and conclusion. AI help is allowed for this part.

Submit one group project report in the Teams folder, with your implementation as supporting material, describing which part of implementation is AI generated. Each team member also need to submit a one-page individual report into the team’s folder, describing the individual contribution to the project (such as the analysis done, ideas tried, part implemented).
